# Project notice

## Purpose and scope

This project implements the support-code format observed in HPE Library & Tape
Tools 6.7. It is intended for maintenance, repair, interoperability research and
preservation of tape hardware that a user owns or is authorized to service.

The generator creates strings. It does not connect to a drive, download or upload
firmware, modify L&TT, or perform a firmware flash. Its output is not an HP or HPE
support authorization, warranty, or certification of firmware compatibility.
